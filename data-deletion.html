Data & Deletion

Algebra Realms Mobile does not create a separate developer-operated account. Google Play Games sign-in is optional.

Remove local game data

On Android, open the device's Settings, find Algebra Realms Mobile under Apps, and choose Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling the game also removes its local data. Clearing or uninstalling may permanently remove progress that has not been saved through Google Play Games.

Google Play Games data

Cloud saves, achievements, leaderboard entries, and the Play Games profile are operated by Google. Use the privacy and data controls in Google Play Games or the player's Google Account to manage that information.

Request help or deletion

Email damiansztajnhof95@gmail.com with the subject “Algebra Realms Mobile data request.” Describe what you want accessed, corrected, or deleted. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or purchase credentials.

We may request enough information to verify and locate the relevant records. We will remove developer-controlled personal data unless it must be retained for a legal obligation, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or another lawful reason. Google-controlled information may need to be handled through Google's account controls or support.

What may remain

Purchase and transaction records may be retained by Google or where required for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, or consumer-protection obligations. Any necessary retention will be limited to the applicable purpose and period.
